// The Merchant Problem

The Chaos of Spreadsheets & Phantom Revenue

Thousands of e-commerce brands and social commerce merchants run operations on fragmented Excel sheets, paper notebooks, and messy WhatsApp chats. They record gross sales but have zero visibility into their real net profit after subtracting product cost, shipping fees, returns, and ad spend.

Furthermore, business owners hesitate to hire operational staff because existing tools either reveal all financial numbers or lack strict role permissions.

// The Product Solution

Real Net Profit & Operational Clarity

Dafaterak replaces complex spreadsheets with an intuitive, mobile-ready merchant hub. It automatically deducts unit costs, courier fees, and marketing spend to calculate true profit in real-time.

Features database-level Row Level Security (RLS) so staff manage orders and fulfillment without ever seeing financial margins or net profits.

// Core Modules

BUILT FOR ACTIVE COMMERCE SELLERS

Live Net Profit Engine

Instant calculation of net earnings per order, per product, and per campaign after all costs, shipping, and returns.

Database-Level Role Permissions

Staff and moderation roles process orders and inventory with revenue figures completely shielded at the database layer.

Courier COD Reconciliation

Track Cash on Delivery status across domestic couriers and automatically reconcile courier statement spreadsheets.

Automated Inventory Warnings

Threshold triggers notify merchants via email and dashboard before top-selling SKUs run out of stock.

Delivery Fleet Tracking

Dedicated module for in-house delivery drivers to view assigned orders and update delivery statuses via mobile.

Ad ROAS & Campaign Tracking

Log paid ad spend across Meta and TikTok to determine true Return on Ad Spend and cost-per-acquisition.
