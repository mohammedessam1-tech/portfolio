// The Business Problem

Slow Response Times & Lost Messaging Sales

In the MENA region, over 70% of e-commerce customer interactions happen on WhatsApp. However, merchants lose up to 40% of prospective buyers simply due to delayed responses during evening peak hours, missed inquiries, and manual order status lookups.

Existing bot solutions are either clunky rule-based trees that frustrate customers or complex enterprise platforms that cost thousands of dollars to configure.

// The Product Solution

Intelligent, Context-Aware WhatsApp Flows

ZBot combines flexible conversational branching with natural language AI models. It understands customer intent in Arabic and English, responds to product inquiries, tracks shipment statuses, and routes hot leads to human agents seamlessly.

Engineered with a visual flow builder allowing non-technical store owners to build interactive conversational funnels in minutes.

// Core Architecture

AUTOMATION & CONVERSATION ENGINE

Visual Flow Builder

Drag-and-drop conversational branching for welcome sequences, FAQs, product recommendations, and order confirmation.

AI Natural Language Fallback

When users type unstructured questions, LLM models understand colloquial dialect and deliver accurate product details.

E-Commerce Sync

Integrates directly with store databases to look up live order tracking numbers and inventory availability instantly.
